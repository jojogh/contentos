**Hook 模式**:把 X 和 Y 连起来看(跨领域连接型,元模式 4)

**标题**:平台把创意产能变成基础设施之后,预算该往哪排

阿里妈妈在服贸会发布万相妙应 Agent 3.0(90+ Skill、20+ 工具、效率 ×10、CTR +20%、加购 +30%、ROI +40%),同一天蓝色光标与 AhaCreator 的合作细节流出——蓝标还为此专门设立了 AI 投资部并完成投资:500 万达人 + 十余个 AI 智能体串起匹配、建联、议价、订单、核对、交付、发布、数据、结算九环节。

把两条连起来看,它们动的是同一笔预算:过去的「设计外包 + 剪辑 + 达人媒介」三笔钱,前两笔正被平台自己的 Agent 吃掉,第三笔正被达人平台的 AI 员工吃掉。

三条具体判断:

1. **砍「产能型」外包,保留「判断型」外包** — 批量出图/混剪/改尺寸平台已做得更便宜;但「这批素材该讲哪个卖点」需要退货原因和差评内容,Agent 还接不了。
2. **省下的钱投到测试频次** — 产能白菜价后瓶颈立刻转移:以前一个月做 20 张图,现在能做 2000 张,但你测不过来。谁先建起测试通量,谁吃到红利。
3. **留一笔预算给「素材结构」而非「素材数量」** — 抖音把普惠小额补贴换成 3000–6000 元梯度大额券,平台在往高客单推,走量型素材结构越来越不划算,信任型素材(测评/对比/拆解)权重上升。

顺带给一个参照系:南通有家做跨境家纺的公司,11 个人管着 932 个「数字员工」、634 家店,每天上新产品链接超 10 万条,设计出稿从 1 天压到 10 秒,虚拟拍摄替代实拍最高降本 95%。这就是全链路 AI 化之后的极限形态——不是「AI 帮了点忙」,而是人只剩下判断的那部分。

拿它对照自己的团队,你能立刻看出两件事:哪些岗位的人力占比还高得不合理,以及瓶颈接下来会转移到哪里。在那个案例里,瓶颈没有消失,只是从「做不出来」变成了「选不准、合规扛不住」。

一句话总结:产能已经不是杠杆了,判断力和测试通量才是。

**Hook 模式**:大部分人用 X 的方式是错的(反常识型,元模式 3 变体)
**赛道**:🎨 创意广告

Competitor ad cloning just became a $99/month product. Here is where the line is.

Wisry launched an "Agentic AdClone" on Product Hunt this week. You paste a store URL, and an agent scans the Meta and TikTok public ad libraries, filters for ads that have been running a long time with many variants and high impressions, then reverse-engineers the structure — hook, pacing, trust signals, CTA — and generates your own branded static and video versions. It orchestrates Grok, Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, KlingAI and Nano Banana under the hood. $99/month, or $49.50 for the first four weeks. The vendor claims +200% average performance and 10x faster shipping. Treat those as vendor numbers, not benchmarks.

Two things are true at once here.

First, this is real leverage. The most expensive part of creative production was never rendering the asset — it was knowing what to make. Lifting the *structure* of an ad that has already survived the auction is a legitimate shortcut, and it is exactly the kind of task a model does well.

Second, the line between "inspired by structure" and "copied asset" is thin, and it is not the tool that enforces it — you are. Reusing a competitor's footage, their actor, or their script beyond the structural pattern carries real copyright and platform-review exposure, and ad platforms are getting better at detecting near-duplicates.

My working rule: borrow the skeleton, never the skin. Deconstruct the hook and the pacing, then rebuild the content with your own product, your own footage, your own claims. If a human watching both ads side by side can tell they are siblings, you have gone too far.

The bigger shift is that creative strategy is becoming commoditised at the tool layer. When everyone can deconstruct the winning structure in an afternoon, structure stops being a moat. What is left is the part no scraper can reach: your proprietary data about your own customers.
